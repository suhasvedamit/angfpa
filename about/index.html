About Us

Welcome to ANG FPA

Trusted Fire Safety Solutions Provider

Established in 2016, ANG FPA Private Limited is a leading manufacturer and trader of fire alarm control panels, fire safety signages, and fire protection accessories. Based in Thane, Maharashtra, we deliver reliable fire safety solutions for residential, commercial, and industrial establishments.

With a commitment to excellence in fire safety, ANG FPA specializes in semi-addressable fire alarm systems, deluge valve control panels, safety signages, manual call points, annunciator panels, and a wide range of fire protection equipment. Our products are designed to meet the most demanding safety standards, ensuring the protection of lives and property across diverse industries including high-rise buildings, factories, hospitals, and commercial complexes.
